# Credits

Kirby & The Amazing Mirror and its original game code, graphics, audio and other assets belong to their respective rights holders, including Nintendo, HAL Laboratory and Flagship. No ownership of those materials is claimed here, and no ROM is provided.

The original [katam project](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/katam) and its contributors created and maintain the matching disassembly/decompilation, asset tooling and recovered source used as this repository's base. Their commit history and author attribution are preserved. The original README's installation and community links remain available.

MatyMeatBoy maintains this combined research and progress repository. New reporting and integration work is AI-assisted. Public forks and independent ports are credited with exact revisions in the research inventory; a reference does not imply that its changes have been imported.

The European continuation incorporates source recovery by Zhengwen Jiang and sh1ftmaker for the object engine, cutscenes, Flamer, Prank, sprite effects and Shotzo. Original contribution authors and history are retained; MatyMeatBoy authored the regional integration, current-symbol adaptations and verification tooling. Historical naming work by jackmgaller and readable collision work by sh1ftmaker are also preserved on explicitly separate research branches, without claiming matching C credit for proposals or assembly fallbacks.

The progress presentation takes inspiration from [MatyMeatBoy/SS2Decomp](https://github.com/MatyMeatBoy/SS2Decomp). The fresh-tip publication helper and regression tests are adapted from [MatyMeatBoy/SS1Decomp](https://github.com/MatyMeatBoy/SS1Decomp). These reporting tools are separate from the matching game source.

The matching compiler comes from [jiangzhengwenjz/agbcc](https://github.com/jiangzhengwenjz/agbcc), branch `new_newlib_pret`. GNU ARM binutils and the other build dependencies retain their own authorship and licenses. See the workflow and metadata provenance for the pinned compiler revision used to generate a report.
